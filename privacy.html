Qanday ma'lumot olamiz

Siz Google orqali kirganingizda Google bizga faqat quyidagilarni beradi:

  • ismingiz;
  • email manzilingiz;
  • profil rasmingiz.

Parolingiz, kontaktlaringiz, fayllaringiz yoki boshqa Google ma'lumotlaringizni olmaymiz.

Ma'lumot qayerda saqlanadi

Bu ma'lumotlar faqat sizning qurilmangizda, brauzerning lokal xotirasida (localStorage) saqlanadi va sizni tanib, profilingizni ko'rsatish uchun ishlatiladi. Ular bizning serverimizga yuborilmaydi, sotilmaydi va uchinchi shaxslarga berilmaydi.

Ma'lumotni o'chirish

Profil rasmini bosib, Chiqish tugmasini bossangiz, ma'lumotlaringiz qurilmangizdan o'chiriladi. Nova'ning Google akkauntingizga kirish ruxsatini myaccount.google.com/permissions sahifasida istalgan vaqt bekor qilishingiz mumkin.

Uchinchi tomon xizmatlari

Kirish uchun Google Identity Services, sayt joylashuvi uchun Netlify, shriftlar uchun Google Fonts ishlatiladi. Ular o'z maxfiylik siyosatlariga ega.

Privacy Policy

When you sign in with Google, Nova receives only your name, email address and profile picture. This data is stored only on your device (browser localStorage) and is used solely to show your profile in the app. It is not sent to our servers, sold, or shared with third parties.

Signing out removes this data from your device. You can revoke Nova's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Nova uses Google Identity Services (sign-in), Netlify (hosting) and Google Fonts, each governed by its own privacy policy. The service is not directed to children under 13.
